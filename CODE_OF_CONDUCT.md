# Code of Conduct

Twin Bench adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and focus criticism on ideas and
code, not people. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing others' private information
are not acceptable in any project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, or elsewhere when representing the project.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at czj030905@outlook.com. Reports are handled confidentially, and
the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban contributors, following the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
